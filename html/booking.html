Help & Guidelines

Packaging & Shipping Policies

Essential tips to ensure your package arrives in pristine condition.

What items are prohibited from express shipment?

Flammable liquids, explosives, narcotics, perishable contraband, currency notes, and unregistered hazardous chemicals cannot be transported via Mithil Courier Services according to civil aviation guidelines.

How should I package fragile glassware or electronics?

Use minimum 2-3 layers of bubble wrap with corner protectors inside a sturdy corrugated cardboard box. Make sure to check the "Special Fragile Handling" option during booking so our sorting personnel tag your package with neon priority badges.

Can I reschedule or cancel a booked pickup?

Yes, you can modify pickup time slots or cancel free of charge up to 30 minutes before your courier agent arrives by contacting our 24/7 support line.
