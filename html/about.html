Our Journey

From Regional Dispatch to Nationwide Logistics Leader

Mithil Courier Services was founded in 2018 with a single objective: eliminating delays and opacity in package logistics. Over the last 8 years, we have expanded from a boutique city courier to an enterprise-grade multimodal logistics company.

Today, our network moves over 50,000 parcels daily across 240+ cities, powered by automated robotic sorting facilities, zero-emission electric delivery fleets, and proprietary real-time GPS tracking.
