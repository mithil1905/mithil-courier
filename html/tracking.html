Frequently Asked Tracking Questions

Answers to common delivery and tracking inquiries.

Where do I find my Tracking ID or AWB Code?

Your Tracking ID is printed at the top of your physical booking slip and sent via SMS and email confirmation (e.g. MCS-XXXXX). If you booked online, check your booking confirmation screen or email inbox.

How frequently is the live GPS milestone updated?

Our sorting hubs and transport fleet utilize IoT scanners and GPS telematics that update package milestones instantly whenever a parcel is scanned at any hub, relay station, or courier delivery van.

What if my package status says 'Attempted Delivery'?

If the delivery agent could not reach the consignee, a free re-delivery attempt is automatically scheduled for the next business morning. You can also contact our 24/7 helpline to schedule a preferred delivery window.
